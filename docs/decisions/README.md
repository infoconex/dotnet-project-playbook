# Architecture Decision Records

Decision records preserve the context, alternatives, decision, and consequences of significant architectural or governance choices. They provide rationale; the normative requirement itself belongs in the [standards](../standards/engineering-principles.md) and [governance](../governance/standards.md) documents.

## Conventions

- Use the filename `NNNN-short-lowercase-title.md` with sequential IDs.
- Include **Status**, **Context**, **Decision**, **Alternatives**, and **Consequences**.
- Preserve accepted records as history. Supersede an earlier record with a new decision and cross-link them rather than silently rewriting rationale.
- Do not store issue checklist progress, CI status, or template acceptance results in decision records.
